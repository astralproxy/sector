# sector
How to Play

Welcome to SECTOR! Launch relentless volleys of kinetic orbs, destroy wandering anomalies, collect Chronal Dust, and upgrade your ship to survive deeper sectors.

🎮 Controls

📱 Mobile Controls (Touch & Swipe)

Auto-Track Mode (Recommended): When AUTO-TRACK is active, your ship automatically tracks and aligns with target anomalies.

Manual Drag (Lower Safe Zone): Touch and drag inside the lower glow area (bottom 20% of the screen) to manually slide your ship left and right.

Pan & Flick Expanse (Upper Screen): Drag or swipe across the upper space viewport to fluidly pan the camera across the infinite cosmic plane.

💻 Desktop / Keyboard Controls

Move Left: Press A or Left Arrow

Move Right: Press D or Right Arrow

Auto-Pilot Toggle: Click the AUTO-TRACK button at the top to toggle between manual and automatic target tracking.

🚀 Game Loop & Mechanics

Fire Orbs: Your ship automatically fires oblong laser orbs into space. Orbs ricochet off boundaries and anomalies, dealing damage on each collision.

Collect Chronal Dust & XP Shards: Destroy anomalies to harvest Chronal Dust (light orange currency) and XP shards. XP shards pull toward your ship via magnetic attraction.

Sector Advancements (In-Run Drafts): Leveling up opens an evolution draft menu where you can choose temporary run upgrades like Pulse Accelerator, Ricochet Matrix, or Multiball Salvo.

Meta Lab Upgrades: Click META LAB at any time to spend your collected Chronal Dust on permanent cross-run upgrades (e.g., Rapid Launcher, Kinetic Conservation, and Armada). Purchases immediately boost your active run stats!

Live Player Stats: Tap the Active Orbs pill at the top of the HUD to toggle the live stats panel and inspect your active weapon telemetry.
